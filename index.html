Privacy Policy

SAJID AI LAB built the Sajid Gold Calculator Pakistan app as a free application. This app is provided for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

We value your privacy. The application is designed to help users calculate, manage, and maintain gold and jewelry-related records.

The app may collect or store the following information for app functionality:

  • Customer Name
  • Customer Phone Number
  • Gold Transaction Records
  • Silver Transaction Records
  • Invoice Details
  • Calculation History
  • Financial Information Entered By The User

This information is used only for app functionality, invoice generation, record management, reporting, printing, and backup features.

Customer Information

When users create invoices or customer records, customer names and phone numbers may be stored locally on the user's device.

This information is not sold to third parties and is used only for business record keeping within the application.

Local Data Storage

Calculations, invoices, customer names, phone numbers, transaction records, reports, backups, and app settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

We do not store this information on our own external servers.

Internet Usage

The application may use an internet connection for:

  • Fetching latest gold and silver prices
  • Loading advertisements
  • Firebase services
  • App diagnostics and analytics
  • Google Play Services

Third-Party Services

The application may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob

These services may collect certain technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Device Information and Identifiers

  • App Version
  • Crash Reports
  • Diagnostics Information
  • App Interaction Data
  • Performance Information
  • Approximate Location (if provided by device or advertising services)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal user information.

Some technical information may be processed by trusted service providers such as:

  • Google AdMob

This processing is required to provide advertising, analytics, diagnostics, security, and application improvements.

Data Security

We value your trust. Data transmitted through Google services is protected using industry-standard security measures and encrypted network connections (HTTPS).

Data Deletion

Users may delete invoices, customer records, and transaction history directly from the application where supported.

Because most information is stored locally on the user's device, uninstalling the application may also remove locally stored app data, depending on device settings.

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Parents or guardians may supervise usage where appropriate.
